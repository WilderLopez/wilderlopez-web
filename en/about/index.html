My Story

I'm a Senior iOS Engineer with 7 years shipping consumer apps across iOS, iPadOS, watchOS and macOS. Today I'm the Tech Lead of a 7-person product team building a consumer social app, where I own the iOS application end to end: architecture, SwiftUI feature delivery, monetization, analytics and App Store releases.

I have deep experience with audio and realtime media playback, multithreading with Swift Concurrency and GCD, and CI/CD automation for build, signing and release. I've taken products from inception to production repeatedly, including my own apps shipped solo on the App Store, and I make product decisions with data: event taxonomy, funnels, retention and A/B tests on pricing and paywalls.

My journey started with Computer Engineering at the University of Informatics Sciences in Havana, Cuba (2014–2019). Since then I've worked across very different products, from a national-scale messaging app and a food delivery driver app to live video surveillance, VoIP telephony and a consumer social network, always holding the bar for quality that the Apple ecosystem demands.
