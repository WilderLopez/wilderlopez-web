3. Subscriptions and Purchases

ClipCard is free to use. Saving, reading, copying, pasting, searching, importing and plain JSON export of your own data are free of charge and are never blocked by a purchase. ClipCard Pro (the paid tier, "Pro") adds: every saved clip reachable from the keyboard instead of 3, a password-protected AES-256-GCM backup export, and three extra themes. No security feature is part of the paid tier.

  • Monthly: $2.99 per month, with a 1 week free trial for new subscribers

Prices are shown in US dollars; the App Store displays the price and currency for your region, and local taxes may apply. All payments are processed by Apple, and we never receive your payment details.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of that period. If you do not cancel a free trial before it ends, it converts into a paid subscription. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings on your device, and cancelling stops future charges without removing access for the period already paid. Refunds are handled by Apple under its own policies.

If a subscription lapses, nothing you saved is taken away or locked. You keep full access to your data, and you can still export all of it. The keyboard returns to carrying 3 clips.
