Kist

The honest antique identifier

iOS 18+ Public Beta
Join the beta on TestFlight

Kist identifies antiques and second-hand finds from a photo and gives you an honest AI value range, never a fake-precise number. But the real product is your collection: save it, annotate it, see its value, and export it. Yours to keep.

Features

  • Honest identification - What it is, era, and materials from a single photo
  • Value as a range - Always a range with confidence, never a fake-precise number
  • A real native collection - Save, edit, search and organize your objects
  • iCloud sync - Your collection across your devices, privately
  • Export CSV / PDF - Your data is yours, take it anywhere
  • Home Screen widget - Your collection's estimated value at a glance
  • Honest paywall - Real free tier, no card required, no dark patterns

The public beta is open. Join it on TestFlight (iOS 18+), free identifications with no card required. App Store release coming soon.
